Who We Are

More Than A Gym.
A Standard.

Space7 is a strength & conditioning gym in the heart of Gulshan-e-Maymar. We don't sell shortcuts — we build athletes, transformations and habits that hold. Whether you're lifting for the first time or chasing a 100-day change, our coaches put a real plan behind every rep.

Coaching, not just floor time

IFA & ACE-certified trainers design programs around your body, goals and lifestyle.

Every level welcome

From inclusive fitness and youth athletes to heavy lifters — there's a lane here for everyone.

Results you can measure

Strength, fat loss, conditioning — tracked progress so you always know it's working.
